The FruitiOS story

From $FruitiOS:> to this.

FruitiOS began with a simple command-line environment. Over time it grew into an experiment in window management, graphics, mouse input, applications and an entirely custom interface.

FruitiOS 2 continues that idea with a more polished, cheerful and modern visual language.
